Applied Scientist at Amazon

I build AI systems that make decisions — from autonomous agents that plan and act, to data-driven methods that enable real-time operations at scale. My work bridges AI agents, LLMs, and data-driven optimization.

Currently an Applied Scientist at Amazon working on seller-facing AI agents. ML PhD from Georgia Tech / NSF AI4OPT with Pascal Van Hentenryck. Our SkillsBench is increasingly adopted as an agentic evaluation by frontier labs (Qwen, HY3), and my work on scalable data-driven decision making has seen major industrial adoption.

Research

My research focuses on enabling AI systems to make complex decisions autonomously and reliably. I am particularly interested in how LLM-based agents can acquire, compose, and execute procedural skills to solve real-world tasks — and how we can verify and trust their decisions.
